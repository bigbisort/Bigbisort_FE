Our Story

Founded in 2024, Bigbisort connects Indian farmers with global buyers through a transparent and efficient online platform. Our mission is to promote fair trade, support sustainable agriculture, and showcase the rich diversity of India's produce to the world.

Vision

To establish a transparent and efficient agriculture export network connecting Indian farmers directly with global buyers.

Mission

To empower Indian farmers through technology and simplify the global sourcing of high-quality produce from India.
